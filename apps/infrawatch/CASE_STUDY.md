# InfraWatch — Product case study

## Problem

During an incident, engineers need a trustworthy overview of system health without parsing several monitoring tools.

## Product decision

InfraWatch creates a clear hierarchy: overall status, live service health, response time and recent incident history.

## Implemented experience

- Overall uptime derived from current service state.
- Interactive service failure and recovery simulation.
- Latency distribution and percentile summary.
- Incident history with duration and resolution state.

## Role and stack

DevOps product UX, information architecture and frontend implementation using React, TypeScript and CSS.

## Suggested validation

Test incident triage scenarios with developers; measure time to identify the failing service and confidence in current status.
