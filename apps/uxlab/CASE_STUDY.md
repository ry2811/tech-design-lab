# UX Lab — Product case study

## Problem

Product teams struggle to connect usability-test setup, participant experience and synthesized insights in one place.

## Product decision

UX Lab shows both sides of research work: an interactive participant prototype and a live evidence summary for the researcher.

## Implemented experience

- Five-step mobile usability-test prototype.
- Task selection, ease rating and completion feedback.
- Study recruitment and completion indicators.
- Synthesized themes, frequency and participant quote.

## Role and stack

UX research workflow, interaction design and frontend implementation using React, TypeScript and CSS.

## Suggested validation

Evaluate with product designers and researchers; measure study setup comprehension and insight findability.
